FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers. If you can't find what you need, reach out anytime.

Our pricing starts at KES 3,000 for a single-page website, KES 6,000 for a standard business package (up to 5 pages), and KES 10,000 for our premium package (up to 8 pages with advanced features). Every package includes custom design, mobile optimization, and website deployment. Domain name and hosting are billed separately based on your preferences.

Most projects are completed within 7–14 days depending on the package and how quickly you provide content and feedback. Basic websites can be ready in as little as 3–5 days, while larger premium projects with custom features may take up to 3 weeks. We always give you a clear timeline upfront.

Yes, we ask that you provide the text content, logo, and any specific images you want on your site. If you don't have everything ready, we can help with guidance on what's needed and even recommend placeholder images from professional stock libraries while you prepare your own content.

Absolutely. Every website we build is fully responsive — meaning it's designed to look and work perfectly on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. Mobile responsiveness isn't an extra; it's included with every single project.

We can help you purchase and set up both domain names and hosting accounts — we handle the entire technical setup for you. These are billed separately at cost (typically around KES 1,000–2,500/year for a domain and KES 2,000–5,000/year for hosting depending on the provider and plan). You retain full ownership of both.

Yes! Each package includes a set number of revision rounds (1 for Basic, 2 for Business, 3 for Premium). A revision is a set of changes sent all at once. Additional revisions after that can be arranged at a small extra charge, and we also offer ongoing monthly maintenance plans.

We work on a simple 50/50 split: 50% upfront to book your project slot and begin work, and the remaining 50% after you've reviewed the final website and given your approval. Payment is accepted via M-Pesa only.

Yes. Our Premium package includes 30 days of free minor support after launch. For continued support, we offer affordable monthly maintenance plans that cover updates, content changes, security monitoring, backups, and technical support. Just ask for details!
